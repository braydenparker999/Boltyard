# Redstone Canyon texture sources

The two 1024px JPGs are unmodified Poly Haven **Rock Surface** texture maps, downloaded September 6, 2026. They are packaged locally; the game makes no network requests.

- Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_surface
- License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license
- Albedo: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rock_surface/rock_surface_diff_1k.jpg
- OpenGL normal: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rock_surface/rock_surface_nor_gl_1k.jpg
- Original MD5: albedo `a7285f02b40e8ed6f15f8e97e357031d`; normal `2243da682fc9181016d0b5bb0b684786`.

The canyon shader adds a warm mineral palette, world-space sedimentary bands and broad variation. Supporting surfaces are native convex rock meshes and the shared heightfield. The reference screenshots guided landscape and vehicle shape; their pixels and logos are not game assets.
