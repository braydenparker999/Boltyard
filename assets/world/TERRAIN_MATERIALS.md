# Bolt Yard original terrain materials

These nine PNG material maps were authored for Bolt Yard v0.4 by deterministic
procedural generation. All pixels are original: no photographs, stock textures,
third-party material libraries, or generated reference images were sampled.

`generate_terrain_materials.py` is the complete reproducible source. It uses
NumPy, SciPy, and Pillow and generates at 1024 × 1024 before filtering to the
512 × 512 delivery resolution. All placement, noise, strata, and fractures use
periodic coordinates so each map repeats seamlessly in both axes. Fixed seeds
preserve the authored material appearance on regeneration.

| Material | Albedo | Normal | Roughness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dry sage and short grass over soil | `terrain_grass.png` | `terrain_grass_normal.png` | `terrain_grass_roughness.png` |
| Gritty compacted earth and small pebbles | `terrain_dirt.png` | `terrain_dirt_normal.png` | `terrain_dirt_roughness.png` |
| Weathered warm gray sandstone | `terrain_rock.png` | `terrain_rock_normal.png` | `terrain_rock_roughness.png` |

Albedo files contain normal sRGB values and should use color texture import.
Normal maps use the OpenGL tangent convention; normal and roughness textures
are data and should not receive sRGB color conversion. Roughness is grayscale,
with values mostly between 0.85 and 0.96. There are no metal or emissive surfaces.

Suggested initial use: one tile per 3–5 world meters for grass and dirt, and
roughly 4–7 meters for exposed stone; enable repeat and mipmaps. Subtle normal
strength is intentional. Blend by terrain slope and local material masks, with
low-frequency world-space tinting to break repetition at valley scale. Rock
strata are aligned horizontally in texture coordinates; use a slope-aware
projection for steep surfaces. Do not gamma-correct albedo a second time.

`terrain_materials.json` records exact filenames, size, and measured mean sRGB
color for convenient shader calibration.

## Original juniper foliage card

`juniper_branch.png` is a 512 × 512 RGBA sRGB botanical cutout generated by
`generate_juniper_branch.py`. It uses original procedural curved branches and
tapered needle clusters, rendered at 1024 × 1024 and antialiased to delivery
size. No source imagery is sampled. The narrow woody root is at bottom center;
the irregular terminal growth points upward. The olive and sage palette uses
subtle brighter tips and exposes fine gaps between branch sprays. Transparent
pixels carry dilated edge color to avoid black fringes under mipmapping.

Use alpha scissor around 0.35–0.45, disabled face culling, roughness around 0.9,
and multiple angled intersecting cards to form crowns. Keep the root at the
branch attachment point when placing cards. This PNG should be clamped rather
than repeated and imported as color with alpha. Foliage is separate from the
nine terrain material maps above.
