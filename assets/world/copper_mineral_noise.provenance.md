# Copperline mineral field

`copper_mineral_noise.png` is original deterministic RGB scalar data generated by
`generate_copper_noise.py` (NumPy seed 104729). It contains three independent,
periodic value-noise fields and no photographic or generated-image source.
The 512×512 data texture is interpreted by the original `copper_ground` and
`copper_sandstone` shaders for gravel density, mineral variation and weathering.
It is imported with mipmaps, no sRGB conversion, and a 512-pixel size cap.

The shaders also reuse the existing original `terrain_rock_photo.png` with its
existing provenance. No third-party environment asset was introduced.

All visible rock silhouettes higher than surface aggregate are the exact closed
convex triangle pieces returned by native `get_crawl_rocks()`. The wash floor
remains at native y=0. Plants, trail stakes and <=18 mm embedded chips are visual
surface dressing and do not provide wheel support. Foliage uses opaque alpha
scissoring, spatial batches and quality-dependent distance culling.
